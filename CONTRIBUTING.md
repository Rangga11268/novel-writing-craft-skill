# Contributing to Novel Writing Craft Engine

We welcome contributions from fiction authors, narrative designers, and AI developers!

## How to Contribute
1. **Adding Reference Guides:** Expand `references/` with genre-specific mechanics (e.g. Hard Sci-Fi worldbuilding, High Fantasy magic systems, Murder Mystery clues).
2. **Refining Templates:** Enhance `templates/` with specialized beat sheets and scene cards.
3. **Improving Diagnostic Scripts:** Extend `scripts/check_pacing.py` to identify additional purple prose, passive voice over-usage, and rhythm clumping.
4. **Submitting PRs:** Ensure all markdown documents follow standard GFM, avoid gendered assumptions, and pass `.github/workflows/validate-skill.yml`.
