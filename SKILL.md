## 2. Core Creative Creed (Strict Anti-AI-Slop Rules)

AI models default to purple prose, emotional labeling, and moralizing conclusions. The agent **MUST** enforce these negative constraints:

1. **Banned Emotional Labeling:** Never write *"dia merasa sedih/takut/marah"*. Replace abstract emotions with visceral somatic responses (cold sweat, pulse spike, jaw clenching).
2. **No Melodramatic Dialogue Tags:** Eliminate words like *"ia mendesis", "ia mencemooh", "ia merintih"*. Default to neutral tags (*"katanya", "tanyanya"*) or ground the line in physical **Action Beats**.
3. **Strict POV Discipline (Anti-Head-Hopping):** In 1st Person or 3rd Person Limited, the camera remains permanently locked behind the POV character's retinas. Never describe what another character feels, thinks, or looks like when out of view.
4. **No Moralizing / Essayistic Summaries:** Never conclude a scene with a philosophical summary (*"Dan malam itu ia belajar..."*). End on a hanging sensory detail, a dilemma, or an irreversible action.
5. **No "As You Know, Bob" Infodumping:** Characters must never explain shared backstory to each other purely to inform the reader.

## 5. Diagnostic Quality Gate (Pre-Finalization Checklist)

Before concluding any chapter or scene draft, execute this verification pass:
- [ ] **Hook Check:** Does the opening line immediately introduce tension, physical action, or intrigue?
- [ ] **Life Value Shift:** Has the scene shifted from $+$ to $-$ (or $-$ to $+$)? If no value shifted, the scene is dead.
- [ ] **POV Lock:** Is there any head-hopping or narration of things the POV character cannot perceive?
- [ ] **Sensory Tally:** Are at least 3 distinct senses (tactile, thermal, olfactory, auditory, visual) engaged?
- [ ] **Dialogue Subtext:** Are characters speaking with subtext and distinct linguistic rhythm rather than plain exposition?
- [ ] **Ending Micro-Hook:** Does the chapter end with an unresolved dilemma or physical action that pulls the reader into the next chapter?
