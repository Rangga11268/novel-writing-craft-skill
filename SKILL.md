## 2. Core Creative Creed (Strict Anti-AI-Slop & Anti-Plot-Hole Rules)

The AI **MUST** enforce these strict negative constraints:

1. **Airtight Causality (The Therefore/But Rule):** Every scene beat must be connected by *"Therefore"* (consequence) or *"But"* (complication). Never string events together with *"And then"* (arbitrary sequence).
2. **No Idiot Plots:** Characters must never act beneath their established intelligence or withhold obvious one-sentence truths purely because the author needs plot drama.
3. **Strict Epistemic Isolation:** Characters can only act on information they have explicitly seen, heard, or deduced within the narrative. Zero authorial knowledge leaks.
4. **Banned Emotional Labeling:** Never write *"dia merasa sedih/takut/marah"*. Replace abstract emotions with visceral somatic responses (cold sweat, pulse spike, jaw clenching).
5. **Banned Thought Verbs (Palahniuk Rule):** Ban words like *knows, realizes, thinks, believes, remembers, notices* (in Indonesian: *tahu bahwa, sadar bahwa, berpikir bahwa*). Unpack them into physical, sensory reality.
6. **Banned AI Translationese (Kalk Harfiah Asing):**
   * Never use *"menemukan dirinya..."* $\rightarrow$ use *"tahu-tahu ia sudah..."* or *"ia mendapati dirinya..."*.
   * Never use *"membuat akal sehat"* $\rightarrow$ use *"masuk akal"*.
   * Never use *"mengambil napas dalam"* $\rightarrow$ use *"menarik napas panjang"*.
   * Never use *"di penghujung hari"* $\rightarrow$ use *"pada akhirnya"* or *"biar bagaimanapun"*.
   * Never use *"mata mereka terkunci"* $\rightarrow$ use *"tatapan mereka bersirobok"* or *"pandangan mereka bertemu"*.
   * Never use *"tidak bisa menahan diri untuk tidak..."* $\rightarrow$ use *"tak kuasa menahan..."*.
   * Never use *"menghembuskan napas yang tak disadari ia tahan"* $\rightarrow$ use *"menghela napas lega"*.
   * Purge pseudo-poetic clichés: *tapestri takdir, relung jiwa terdalam, simfoni keheningan, tarian bayang-bayang*.
7. **Natural Dialogue vs. Literary Narrative:**
   * Narrative uses flowing literary standard (*me-, ber-, di-, ter-*, precise imagery).
   * Dialogue uses authentic conversational register (*lu-gue, aku-kamu*, elipsis, *nge-, -in*).
   * Partikel wacana (*sih, kan, dong, deh, kok*) must be used functionally and sparingly (max 1 per clause), never spammed.
8. **Anti-Subtitle & Anti-Robot Dialogue Discipline (MANDATORY DEFAULT):**
   * **Banned Subtitle / Anime Phrasing:** Never translate foreign conversational idioms literally into dialogue (e.g., ❌ *"Lu baru saja menghindar dari tangan gue?"* $\rightarrow$ ✅ *"Belagu amat lu, nyingkir pas gue sentuh"*).
   * **Banned Theatrical / Bookish Phrasing:** Never write dictionary-style single-word refusals (❌ *"Tolak."* / *"Saya menolak."* $\rightarrow$ ✅ *"Ogah."* / *"Gak sudi."* / *"Gak bakal."*).
   * **Banned Robotic Geek Speak in Dialogue:** Smart/geek characters must NEVER talk like an academic paper or walking textbook under stress (❌ *"Cengkeraman ini melanggar ruang batas fisik"* $\rightarrow$ ✅ *"Lepas. Sakit."*; ❌ *"Dan itu tidak membuatmu lebih pintar satu bit pun"* $\rightarrow$ ✅ *"Dan itu gak bikin lu lebih pinter dari gue"*).
   * **Banned Pastiche Honorifics:** No fantasy clan terms in real-world urban settings (❌ *"Bukan pesuruh klan Abimana"* $\rightarrow$ ✅ *"Bukan babu lu atau keluarga lu"*).
   * **Register Consistency in Conflict:** Peers in urban conflict NEVER use polite formal *"Saya - Kamu"*. Use *lu-gue* or omit pronouns entirely.
9. **No Melodramatic Dialogue Tags:** Eliminate words like *"ia mendesis", "ia mencemooh", "ia merintih"*. Default to neutral tags (*"katanya", "tanyanya"*) or ground the line in physical **Action Beats**.
10. **Strict POV Discipline (Anti-Head-Hopping):** In 1st Person or 3rd Person Limited, the camera remains permanently locked behind the POV character's retinas. Never describe what another character feels, thinks, or looks like when out of view.
11. **No Moralizing / Essayistic Summaries:** Never conclude a scene with a philosophical summary (*"Dan malam itu ia belajar..."*). End on a hanging sensory detail, a dilemma, or an irreversible action.
12. **No "As You Know, Bob" Infodumping:** Characters must never explain shared backstory to each other purely to inform the reader.

## 5. Diagnostic Quality Gate (Pre-Finalization Checklist)

Before concluding any chapter or scene draft, execute this verification pass:
- [ ] **Author Intent & Guardrail Check:** Are all user-specified Must-Haves included, and are all Dealbreakers/Banned Tropes strictly absent?
- [ ] **Beban & Bakti Anchor:** Is the high-concept conflict grounded in an intimate filial, economic, or community stake (Tere Liye / JS Khairen principle)?
- [ ] **Humanizer Polish Check:** Are connector bloat (e.g. *Selain itu, Di sisi lain, Oleh karena itu*), nominalization light verbs (*melakukan pengamatan*), and anticipatory negations (*"bukan berarti"*) eradicated?
- [ ] **Sentence Metronome Variation:** Is sentence cadence dynamic (StdDev $\ge 6.0$ words), mixing punchy staccato bursts with expansive periodic clauses?
- [ ] **Plot Hole / Logic Check:** Do all beats connect via *"Therefore"* or *"But"*? Does any character act beneath their intelligence (Idiot Plot)?
- [ ] **Epistemic Source Validated:** Does the POV character have a verified in-story source for every secret or piece of data they speak or act upon?
- [ ] **Spatial & Continuity Check:** Are travel times realistic? Are physical injuries and carried inventory consistent with previous chapters?
- [ ] **Chekhov Setup Check:** If an artifact, skill, or ally appears to solve a crisis, was it planted and reinforced earlier?
- [ ] **Genre Obligatory Scene Met:** Does this chapter fulfill its genre contract (e.g., Fair Play clue in Mystery, Proof of Sacrifice in Romance, Measurable Growth in Progression)?
- [ ] **Word Count Check:** Is the chapter within the optimal reading window (2,000–3,500 words, ~10–14 mins)?
- [ ] **Acoustic Read-Aloud Test:** Does dialogue sound like a living human speaking? Has all mechanical translationese ("membuat akal sehat", "menemukan dirinya") been purged?
- [ ] **Subtext & Iceberg Evasion:** Is the emotional core communicated through evasion, mundane banter, silence, or deflection rather than on-the-nose theatrical confessions?
- [ ] **The Tagless Blind Test:** Can you obscure all dialogue tags and immediately identify each speaker by syntax, vocabulary, and rhythm?
- [ ] **Register Congruence & Code-Switching:** Does dialogue register match social hierarchy, intimacy level, and bystander presence?
- [ ] **Two "Thisness" Anchors:** Does the scene contain at least two idiosyncratic, tactile sensory details reflecting physical and socio-economic reality?
- [ ] **Sociolect Consistency:** Do characters speak in their proper social register (urban slang, academic, or working-class survival)?
- [ ] **Life Value Shift:** Has the scene shifted from $+$ to $-$ (or $-$ to $+$)? If no value shifted, the scene is dead.
- [ ] **POV Lock:** Is there any head-hopping or narration of things the POV character cannot perceive?
- [ ] **Thought Verbs Purged:** Are verbs like *realized, thought, knew, felt* unpacked into concrete physical sensory actions?
- [ ] **Sensory Tally:** Are at least 3 distinct senses (tactile, thermal, olfactory, auditory, visual) engaged?
- [ ] **Ending Micro-Hook (Zeigarnik Open Loop):** Does the chapter end with an unresolved dilemma or physical action that pulls the reader into the next chapter?
