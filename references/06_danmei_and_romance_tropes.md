# Danmei, BL & Romance Narrative Tropes

A deep-dive guide into the emotional mechanics of modern Danmei (耽美), Boys' Love (BL), and contemporary Western/Asian romance structures.

---

## 1. The Anatomy of "火葬场" (Zhuī Qī Huǒ Zàng Chǎng - Wife-Chasing Crematorium)

*Literally: "Chasing your wife through the crematorium."*
Originating in Chinese web literature, this trope depicts an arrogant, callous, or abusive male lead who takes the protagonist for granted, only to suffer agonizing regret when the protagonist severs all ties.

### The 4 Standard Stages:
1. **The Arrogance & Imbalance Phase:**
   * The Gong/ML wields overwhelming power (wealth, social status, physical dominance).
   * The Shou/MC endures coldness or mistreatment due to love, debt, or lack of power.
2. **The "Heart-Death" Point (心死 - Xīn Sǐ):**
   * The turning point where the MC realizes the Gong will never change or respects no boundaries.
   * The MC stops crying, arguing, or begging. They become icy, polite, and completely emotionally detached.
3. **The Clean Severance / Disappearance:**
   * The MC leaves cleanly (often leaving all gifts/money behind, moving abroad, or cutting all communication).
   * The Gong discovers an empty room, a broken contact card, or a paid-off debt receipt.
4. **The Crematorium Agony:**
   * The Gong suffers public humiliation, self-destruction, sleeplessness, and psychological torment trying to retrieve the MC.

## 2. "换攻" (Huàn Gōng - Gong Replacement) & Failed Crematorium

In classic crematoriums, the Gong eventually redeems himself. In **"换攻" (Huan Gong)**, however, the narrative refuses redemption:

* **The Principle of Failed Crematorium (换攻火葬场):**
  * The initial Gong (渣攻 - Scum Gong) has committed unforgivable emotional or physical violations (e.g. crossing consent, exploiting family vulnerabilities, structural betrayal).
  * His "crematorium" groveling fails completely. The MC never looks back.
* **The Entrance of the True Male Lead (正攻 - Zheng Gong / Gong 2):**
  * A healthier, emotionally mature, and genuinely protective partner enters the story.
  * Contrast: Where Gong 1 demanded submission, Gong 2 offers respect. Where Gong 1 brought anxiety, Gong 2 provides a safe harbor.
  * **The Ultimate Catharsis:** Gong 1 is forced to watch from afar as the MC flourishes and loves Gong 2 openly.

## 3. Straight-to-Awakened & Demisexual Mechanics

When a character considers themselves heterosexual but falls in love with someone of the same gender:

1. **Avoid the "Sudden Personality Flip":**
   * A straight character does not wake up one morning universally attracted to all men.
   * The attraction must be **person-specific**: it is rooted in intellectual admiration, unprecedented emotional safety, and physical chemistry specific to that individual.
2. **The Cognitive Dissonance ("Gay Panic"):**
   * The character attempts to rationalize their physiological reactions using their usual straight logic:
     * *"I’m just admiring his physique as an athlete."*
     * *"My heart is racing because the room is too warm."*
3. **The Surrender (Awakening):**
   * The realization that labels are secondary to the undeniable reality of this human connection:
     * *"I am not into guys. I am hopelessly, irreversibly in love with you."*
