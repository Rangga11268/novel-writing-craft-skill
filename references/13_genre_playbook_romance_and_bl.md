# Genre Playbook: Romance, BL & Danmei

A definitive, industrial-grade craft manual for writing bestselling contemporary Romance, Boys' Love (BL), and modern/historical Danmei (耽美).

---

## 1. The Core Emotional Contract (The Sacred Promise)

* **Primary Human Need:** *Connection, Belonging, and Intimacy vs. Isolation.*
* **The Non-Negotiable Contract:** 
  * Every commercial romance **MUST end in a HEA (Happily Ever After)** or at minimum a **HFN (Happy For Now)**. Ending with unprompted tragedy without an explicit "Tragedy" tag is considered a fatal breach of contract by romance readers.
  * **The True Currency of Love:** Love is only validated when both characters voluntarily dismantle their strongest psychological defense mechanisms (*The Lie Believed*) and surrender to emotional vulnerability.

---

## 2. Sub-genre Mechanics & Specific Tropes

### A. Danmei & The "Crematorium" (追妻火葬场 - Zhuī Qī Huǒ Zàng Chǎng)
* **The Core Appeal:** Retributive emotional justice (*Moral Catharsis*).
* **The Dynamic:** An arrogant, powerful, or emotionally negligent male lead (*Scum Gong / 渣攻*) takes the protagonist (*Shou / 受*) for granted.
* **The "Heart-Death" (心死 - Xīn Sǐ):** The turning point where the Shou stops crying, arguing, or asking for affection. He becomes polite, detached, and cold.
* **The Failed Crematorium (换攻火葬场 - Huàn Gōng):** If Gong 1 committed unforgivable structural or bodily violations, the groveling fails completely. Gong 2 (*Zheng Gong / 正攻*) steps in as a healthy, protective partner, and Gong 1 is left with eternal regret.

### B. Enemies-to-Lovers & Forced Proximity
* **Friction to Heat:** Sexual tension is generated from intense opposition. The characters are obsessed with each other long before they admit to attraction.
* **Key Bestselling Tropes:**
  * *The "Who Did This To You?" (Protector Rage):* A cold, restrained rival erupts into uncontrollable fury when a third party injures their rival.
  * *Only One Bed / Stranded Together:* Stripping away external social roles and forcing raw physical intimacy.

### C. Omegaverse (ABO)
* **The Core Conflict:** Biological determinism (*pheromone compulsion, rut, heat*) vs. pure emotional agency.
* **Bestselling Angle:** Defective scent glands or mismatched pheromones that are socially ridiculed, but produce unprecedented soul-level resonance between the two leads.

## 3. Obligatory Scenes (Story Grid Framework)

Every romance novel must contain these 8 structural pillars:

```
[1. Society Boundary] ──► [2. Volatile Collision] ──► [3. Forced Enclosure] ──► [4. First Crack / Kiss]
                                                                                        │
[8. Recommitment/HEA] ◄── [7. Penance / The Chase] ◄── [6. The Breakup Crisis] ◄────────┘
                                                         (Crematorium Trigger)
```

1. **Society / Contextual Boundary Established:** The social, professional, economic, or biological barrier that makes their union impossible.
2. **The Meet-Cute / Volatile Collision:** The initial spark; an encounter loaded with immediate friction and subconscious fascination.
3. **The Forced Enclosure:** An external event (assignment, storm, contract, mutual debt) traps them in shared physical space.
4. **The First Crack (Point of No Return):** The first voluntary breach of armor (first kiss, confession of a secret wound, or tender caretaking).
5. **The Proof of Sacrifice:** One partner gives up status, money, or physical safety for the other with zero expectation of repayment.
6. **The All-Hope-Is-Lost Crisis (The Crematorium Trigger):** A hidden betrayal or catastrophic misunderstanding surfaces. The wounded partner completely severs ties (*Emotional Detachment*).
7. **The Crucible of Penance (The Grovel / Chase):** The offending partner endures proportional public and psychological torment (burning in the crematorium) to prove irreversible transformation.
8. **The Climax & Recommitment:** Mutual surrender of ego; vows of permanent equality and shared future (HEA).

## 4. Fatal Pitfalls in Romance & BL

* ❌ **The "Cheap Crematorium" (Penebusan Obral):** The Gong abuses the Shou for 60 chapters, but gets forgiven after catching a mild cold or apologizing for 2 chapters. Penebusan dosa **harus sebanding** dengan rasa sakit yang ditimbulkan.
* ❌ **The Idiot Plot (Miscommunication Trap):** Dragging out a conflict for 150 pages that two rational adults could solve in 30 seconds if one didn't walk away mid-sentence.
* ❌ **Pheromone-Only Attraction:** Characters in ABO who have zero conversational chemistry, shared values, or mutual respect, relying 100% on biological gland scent.
* ❌ **Romanticizing Abuse Without Consequence:** Presenting criminal stalking, physical violence, or non-consensual violations as "proof of passionate love" without moral reckoning or character consequence.
