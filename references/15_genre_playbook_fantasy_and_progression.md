# Genre Playbook: Fantasy, Progression, Isekai & LitRPG

A definitive, industrial-grade craft manual for writing bestselling Fantasy, Regression/Reincarnation, Magic System Progression, System Apocalypses, and LitRPG sagas.

---

## 1. The Core Emotional Contract (The Sacred Promise)

* **Primary Human Need:** *Impotence to Omnipotence / Helplessness to Mastery / Ignorance to Cosmic Agency.*
* **The Non-Negotiable Contract:**
  * **The Dopamine Loop of Measurable Growth:** Readers crave the visceral satisfaction of earned, quantified progression (*status screen evolution, cultivation realm breakthroughs, skill fusions*).
  * **Competence & Meritocracy:** Power must feel earned through ruthless discipline, tactical ingenuity, or exploiting hidden system rules—never through blind, unearned luck without risk.
  * **The Catharsis of Retribution ("Face-Slapping" / 打脸 - Dǎ Liǎn):** The corrupt nobles, arrogant elders, or former betrayers who ridiculed the underdog protagonist are eventually forced to bow in public astonishment.
  * **The Reversal of Regret (Regression):** Using future knowledge to prevent past tragedies and save fallen companions.

---

## 2. Sub-genre Mechanics & Specific Tropes

### A. The "Trash" Class with Broken Synergies
* **The Underdog Subversion:** Society labels the protagonist's class or skill as useless (*F-rank Farmer, Necromancer, Support Buffer, Blacksmith*).
* **The Exploit:** The protagonist discovers an overlooked mathematical interaction or ancient synergy that breaks the system rules wide open.

### B. Regression & Future Resource Hoarding
* **Second-Chance Knowledge:** The protagonist dies in an apocalyptic future and wakes up 10 years earlier on the day of the tutorial.
* **Strategic Foreknowledge:** Knowing the exact time, coordinates, and hidden triggers to claim divine artifacts before the villainous conglomerates arrive.

### C. The Blue Screen & The Magic Tier Bottleneck
* **System Windows:** Status screens, level-up notifications, and item tooltips with rich environmental lore.
* **The Conceptual Breakthrough (Tribulation):** Pure level grinding stops at a bottleneck; the character must achieve a philosophical or moral epiphany to ascend to the next realm.

## 3. Obligatory Scenes (Story Grid Framework)

Every fantasy/progression novel must contain these 8 structural pillars:

```
[1. Genesis / Regression] ──► [2. The Underdog Stigma] ──► [3. The Exploit Discovered]
                                                                        │
[8. Apotheosis / Breakthrough] ◄── [7. Cataclysmic Raid] ◄── [6. The Conceptual Wall] ◄── [4. First Solo Crucible]
                                                                        │
                                                              [5. Public Arena Spectacle]
```

1. **The Catalyst & Genesis:** The moment of regression, reincarnation, or the sudden global arrival of the System (*System Apocalypse Global Prompt*).
2. **The Underdog Stigma:** Society, the magic crystal, or the guild appraises the protagonist as "trash" or "F-rank", setting the baseline benchmark.
3. **The Secret Synergy / Exploit Discovered:** The protagonist figures out a loophole in the skill tree or cultivates an ancient forbidden manual.
4. **The First Solo Crucible:** A lethal, solo life-or-death battle against an overwhelming beast or rival, validating that the build is functional.
5. **The Public Tournament / Arena Spectacle:** A public examination (magic academy, arena, dungeon raid) where the protagonist shatters the measuring orb and stuns the sneering elites.
6. **The System Bottleneck / Conceptual Wall:** Number grinding fails against a transcendent barrier; the protagonist must risk their life or sacrifice an aspect of ego to cross it.
7. **The Cataclysmic Raid / Climax Showdown:** A multi-stage tactical battle against an apocalyptic boss where every acquired skill and alliance is mobilized.
8. **Apotheosis & Realm Breakthrough:** The cosmic ascension; new title awarded, stats jump, and the protagonist’s status is permanently rewritten in the universe.

## 4. Fatal Pitfalls in Fantasy & Progression

* ❌ **Power Creep & Number Inflation (Dragon Ball Syndrome):** Damage numbers jump from 150 to 100,000,000 in two volumes. When numbers lose concrete comparative stakes, all tension dies.
* ❌ **Unearned Gary Stu / Cheats Without Risk:** The protagonist is handed omnipotent cheat skills by a goddess with zero sacrifice, zero mental effort, and zero physical risk, destroying the core satisfaction of "Progression".
* ❌ **Inventory & Skill Amnesia:** The author awards 35 cool skills in Act I, but completely forgets 30 of them in Act II because they cannot track their own system.
* ❌ **Brain-Dead Antagonists (Young Master Caricatures):** Villains who exist only to scream *"How dare you!"* like petulant toddlers with zero rational cunning. Intelligent, competent antagonists are mandatory for satisfying face-slaps.
* ❌ **Violating Hard Magic Rules (System Inconsistency):** Breaking the established mechanics via authorial fiat to bail out the protagonist when they are tactically cornered.
