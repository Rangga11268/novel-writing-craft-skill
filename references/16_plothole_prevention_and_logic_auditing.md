# Plot Hole Prevention, Continuity & Narrative Logic Auditing

A master developmental editing manual for detecting, preventing, and repairing plot holes, logic fractures, and narrative inconsistencies across long-form fiction.

---

## 1. Taxonomy of the 7 Fatal Plot Holes

A plot hole is not merely an unpopular creative choice; it is an **internal consistency breach** where the story violates its own established rules of physics, logic, causality, character competence, or information access.

```
┌───────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PLOT HOLE TYPE                    │ CORE MECHANISM OF FAILURE                              │
├───────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Causal Breaks (Non-Sequitur)   │ Event B occurs without adequate cause A; broken chain. │
│ 2. Character Inconsistency / Idiot│ Character acts dumb/OOC purely to advance the plot.    │
│ 3. Temporal-Spatial Errors        │ Narrative teleportation; timeline & travel conflicts.  │
│ 4. Information Asymmetry Leaks    │ POV contamination; character knows unearned secrets.   │
│ 5. Worldbuilding Rule Violations  │ Violating hard magic/tech limits established earlier.  │
│ 6. Deus Ex Machina                │ Crisis solved by unearned luck/external intervention.  │
│ 7. Dropped Threads (Orphan Guns)  │ Major clues, artifacts, or mysteries left abandoned.   │
└───────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Core Narrative Logic Frameworks

### A. The "Therefore / But" Causality Rule (Matt Stone & Trey Parker)
Every beat in your outline or chapter progression must be connected by causal conjunctions, never arbitrary sequence:

* ❌ **The "And Then" Trap (Episodic Sprawl):** Scene A happens, *and then* Scene B happens, *and then* Scene C happens. Events merely follow in chronological sequence without organic cause-and-effect.
* ✅ **The "Therefore / But" Engine (Causal Momentum):**
  * **[ACTION] $\rightarrow$ THEREFORE $\rightarrow$ [DIRECT FALLOUT]:** Every choice has a logical, unavoidable reaction.
  * **[FALLOUT] $\rightarrow$ BUT $\rightarrow$ [NEW OBSTACLE / COMPLICATION]:** The reaction triggers an unexpected friction that escalates the stakes.

### B. The Setup & Payoff Matrix (Chekhov's Gun - The Rule of Three)
Every critical weapon, spell, piece of evidence, or ally that saves the day in Act III must follow the **3-Phase Lifecycle**:
1. **Phase 1: Planting (Act I):** Introduced casually in an everyday context so it blends into the background without drawing undue suspicion.
2. **Phase 2: Reinforcing (Act II):** Briefly validated or tested in a minor obstacle to remind the reader of its existence and operational rules.
3. **Phase 3: Payoff (Act III):** Deployed as the decisive linchpin during the climactic crisis.

| Audit Quadrant | Payoff Present (+) | Payoff Absent (-) |
|---|---|---|
| **Setup Present (+)** | **NARRATIVE GOLD:** Earned, satisfying catharsis. | **DROPPED THREAD (Orphan Gun):** Broken reader promise. |
| **Setup Absent (-)** | **DEUS EX MACHINA / ASSPULL:** Unearned cheat. | **DEAD WEIGHT:** Clutter to be excised during revision. |

### C. The "Why Don't They Just...?" 4-Gates Plausibility Stress-Test
Whenever a character chooses a difficult, dangerous, or convoluted path, run their choice through the **Four Gates of Narrative Plausibility**:

1. **Gate 1: Knowledge Gate:** Does the character actually know the easier option exists? *(If no, ensure their ignorance is plausible, not contrived).*
2. **Gate 2: Capability Gate:** Does the character possess the physical stamina, time, tools, or resources to execute the easy option? *(e.g. Dead phone battery, washed-out bridge, storm).*
3. **Gate 3: Psychological & Ethical Gate:** Does the character's internal trauma, moral code, ego, or fear forbid the easy option? *(e.g. Refusing police help because of past police betrayal).*
4. **Gate 4: Consequence & Cost Gate:** Does taking the easy shortcut unleash a catastrophe far worse than the present danger? *(e.g. Flying the Eagles into Mordor exposes the Ring to the Eye of Sauron).*

> 🚨 **Plot Hole Warning:** If an easier solution passes all 4 Gates without a valid barrier, **you have an Idiot Plot**. Revise the obstacle immediately.

## 3. The 6-Layer Developmental Editor Anti-Plot-Hole Checklist

Execute this checklist during revision before finalizing any novel manuscript:

### Layer 1: Macro-Causality & Plot Spine
- [ ] Are all major plot milestones connected exclusively by *"Therefore"* or *"But"*, with zero *"And then"* links?
- [ ] Would the entire central conflict evaporate if two characters sat down and spoke honestly for 10 minutes? *(If yes, deepen external stakes or add legitimate secrets to kill the Idiot Plot).*
- [ ] Is the final victory 100% earned by the protagonist's active decisions and sacrifices, rather than luck or coincidence?

### Layer 2: Character Competence & Agency
- [ ] Does any character act uncharacteristically foolish or out-of-character purely to get captured or trigger an action scene?
- [ ] Is the antagonist operating with equal or greater intelligence than the protagonist? Is their master plan logically sound from their perspective?

### Layer 3: Epistemic Tracking & Information Asymmetry
- [ ] **Knowledge Origin Check:** For every major secret revealed: *In which specific chapter did this character see, hear, or read this information for the first time?*
- [ ] **POV Integrity:** Has any thought, physiological sensation, or secret of a non-POV character leaked into a Third-Person Limited scene?
- [ ] **No Convenient Amnesia:** Does the protagonist remember crucial tools, allies, or warnings received earlier when entering danger?

### Layer 4: Spatial & Temporal Logistics
- [ ] **Travel Velocity Check:** Have travel times between cities/locations accounted for realistic speeds, terrain, weather, and rest periods? (No narrative teleportation).
- [ ] **Calendar & Weather Lock:** Are seasons, day/night transitions, days of the week, and weather patterns strictly continuous across chapter breaks?
- [ ] **Injury Continuity:** Do physical wounds have realistic recovery timelines? (A character shot in the leg in Chapter 8 cannot sprint a marathon in Chapter 10).

### Layer 5: Chekhov Setup & Payoff Audit
- [ ] Has every weapon, skill, or ally used in the final 20% of the book been seeded at least twice in the first 50%?
- [ ] Have all orphan subplots, unresolved side-mysteries, and introduced artifacts been either resolved or explicitly tagged for a sequel?

### Layer 6: Devil's Advocate Stress-Test
- [ ] Has the *"Why Don't They Just...?"* 4-Gates test been executed on the story's 3 most controversial decisions?
- [ ] **Authority Intervention Check:** Why hasn't the government, police, military, or higher guild authority solved this problem? Is the narrative isolation completely airtight?
