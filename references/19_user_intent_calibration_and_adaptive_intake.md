# 19. USER INTENT CALIBRATION & ADAPTIVE INTAKE PROTOCOL

> *"The fatal mistake of AI creative tools is assuming it knows what the user wants after a one-line prompt. A master developmental editor never writes in the dark; they interview the author until the soul of the story is locked."*

---

## 🧭 Executive Summary

Every author arrives with a different vision. One wants a cozy slice-of-life romance with zero angst; another wants an adrenaline-pumping grimdark thriller with body horror; a third wants a serial webnovel engineered for daily cliffhangers on Royal Road or Wattpad.

This module provides the **Adaptive User Intent Intake Protocol**: an active diagnostic interviewing system that calibrates the AI's creative engine to match the author's exact desires, constraints, and platform goals **before** any chapter or outline is drafted.

---

## 🚦 Part 2: The Two Intake Tracks

The AI adapts its interview based on user preference:

### Track A: The 5-Minute "Express Intake" (For Fast Spark Sessions)
When a user wants to move quickly without deep paperwork, the AI prompts only these 5 questions:

1. **The Logline / Premise:** What happens, to whom, and what is the core obstacle?
2. **The Genre & Tropes:** What genre is this, and what are 2 tropes you love (or must have)?
3. **The Anti-Tropes (Dealbreakers):** What tropes do you **hate** and must never appear? (e.g., *no cheating, no love triangles, no dead pets, no insta-love*).
4. **Tone & Ending:** Do you want this cozy, dark, or balanced? Must the ending be happy (HEA)?
5. **Target Readership:** Who is this for? (e.g., Wattpad readers, serious literary fiction, binge webnovel, etc.).

## 🧠 Part 3: How the Engine Dynamically Adapts

Once the user's intent is established, the AI dynamically reconfigures its internal prompt behavior:

| Intake Parameter | Engine Auto-Adjustment |
|---|---|
| **Platform = Wattpad / Webnovel** | Compresses chapter opening (Action Hook within 150 words), increases dialogue ratio to 40%–50%, forces high-stakes Zeigarnik cliffhangers on line 2,200. |
| **Platform = Kindle Unlimited Thriller** | Enforces Knox's Decalogue Fair Play, calibrates a ticking clock under 72 hours, locks POV strictly to 1st Person or 3rd Limited. |
| **Platform = Literary / Metropop** | Dilates sensory "thisness" (James Wood's Haecceity), increases internal monologue depth, slows pacing for poignant subtext, prioritizes authentic Indonesian sociolects. |
| **Tone = Cozy Slice-of-Life** | Eliminates mortal peril, amplifies sensory descriptions of comfort (tea, food, warm fabric), resolves interpersonal misunderstandings through gentle communication. |
| **Tone = Grimdark / Horror** | Amplifies 3-layer somatic dread, visceral biological details, procedural institutional corruption, and pyrrhic moral sacrifices. |
| **Dealbreaker: "No Miscommunication"** | Completely disables the *Idiot Plot* engine; conflicts must arise from irreconcilable core values or external opposing forces, never from unshared secrets. |

## 🔄 Part 4: The Intent Alignment Confirmation Protocol

Before generating an outline or chapter, the AI must output an **Intent Alignment Anchor** to the user:

```text

• Genre & Hybrid: [e.g., Indonesian Cyberpunk / BL Slow Burn]

• Target Platform: [e.g., Serial Webnovel / Kindle]

• Ending Contract: [e.g., Guaranteed HEA with high vindication]

Proceeding to [Plot Architect / Scene Drafter] under these exact constraints.
```
