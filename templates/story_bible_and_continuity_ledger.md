## 3. Character Knowledge State Matrix (K-State Ledger)

Track who knows what, when, and how, to prevent **Information Asymmetry Leaks** (POV Bleed):

* **Knowledge Scale:**
  * `0 = UNKNOWN` (Has never heard or observed it).
  * `1 = HEARSAY` (Heard a rumor; unconfirmed).
  * `2 = SUSPECTED` (Deduces it from indirect circumstantial evidence).
  * `3 = VERIFIED FACT` (Direct witness, indisputable proof).
  * `4 = DECEIVED` (Firmly believes false information/misdirection).

| Fact / Secret (ID & Description) | Protagonist (Danu) | Antagonist (Satria) | Ally / Love Interest (Adrian) | Reveal Chapter & Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FACT-01:** Danu is accepted to MIT with full scholarship | **Status: 3** (Ch. 12 via secret email) | **Status: 0** (Ch. 1–15)<br>**Status: 3** (Ch. 16 post-escape) | **Status: 3** (Ch. 17 as lab mentor) | Revealed to Satria only after Danu's plane takes off. *Satria must NOT know before Ch. 16!* |
| **FACT-02:** Satria's father took bribes from exam contractor | **Status: 0** (Ch. 1–8)<br>**Status: 2** (Ch. 9 via server log)<br>**Status: 3** (Ch. 14 decryption) | **Status: 3** (Known from family) | **Status: 0** (Irrelevant to Adrian) | Danu discovers this in Ch. 14; uses it as insurance for grandmother's protection. |
